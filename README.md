# Awesome Tools & Resources for OnlyFans Creators & Agencies

I worked in the OnlyFans space for 4 years managing at peak 32 active creators handling the Social Media Marketing & Strategy for creators. Over the years I've used hunndreds of tools, software and built out systems. 

Since retiring from the space, I decided to document and share a load of databases, tools and knowledge that I've collected over the years in a hope it servers you as well as it has me.

## Data & AI

🧩 **Management Templates**

- **Airtable Management Template** — ready-made Airtable base for running agency ops.
- **Notion Management Template** — ready-made Notion workspace for dashboards, SOPs and agency management.

🔌 **Account Scraping, APIs and Automation**

- **[RapidAPI](https://rapidapi.com)** — API marketplace; where we source most of the scraper APIs below.
- **TikTok Scraper API** — scrape TikTok profiles, videos and trend data at scale.
- **Instagram Scraper API** — scrape Instagram profiles, posts and follower data.
- **OnlyFans Scraper API** — pull OnlyFans profile and content data.
- **Twitter Scraper API** — scrape Twitter/X profiles, tweets and engagement data.
- **Reddit Scraper API** — scrape subreddits, posts and comment threads.
- **Apify** — web scraping and automation platform; used to build and run custom scrapers (e.g. trend and content data pipelines) at scale.

⚙️ **Automation**

- **[Make](https://www.make.com)** — no-code workflow automation; glues scrapers, sheets and notifications together.
- **[n8n](https://n8n.io)** — self-hostable workflow automation; more control, no per-task pricing.
  

🤖 **AI Tools**

- **[Codex](https://github.com/openai/codex)** — OpenAI's coding agent; builds and maintains our internal tools.
- **[Claude Code](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code)** — Anthropic's CLI coding agent; same job, different model.
- **[Nano Banana Pro](https://gemini.google.com/)** — Google's Gemini image generation/editing model; content and promo image work.
- **[ComfyUI](https://github.com/comfyanonymous/ComfyUI)** — node-based image generation pipeline (Stable Diffusion/Flux); custom content workflows.
- **[ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io)** — AI voice generation and cloning; voice notes and audio content.

🔍 **Duplicate Content Detection**

- **[AugLy](https://github.com/facebookresearch/AugLy)** — Meta's open-source data augmentation library on GitHub; used to detect near-duplicate and lightly-modified reposts of content.
